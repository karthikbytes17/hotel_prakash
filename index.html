About the Hotel

About Hotel Prakash

Celebrated as one of Karkala's most trusted lodging options, Hotel Prakash Boarding & Lodging is strategically located in the heart of Karkala town.

This hospitality destination has grown to become the ideal place for travellers and business visitors to feel at home when in Karkala. Whether you're visiting the area's famous Jain temples, the iconic Gommateshwara statue, or attending to business in town — Hotel Prakash offers comfortable, clean and value-for-money accommodation.

Rooms are equipped with the essentials you need — hot water, TV, clean linen, towels and bathing soaps. The in-house veg restaurant, with its separate AC dining section, keeps guests well-fed with fresh, tasty and reasonably priced food. A separate kitchen now serves non-veg dishes too.
